# Success Online logo assets

The website uses the supplied Success Online Services S-and-globe identity in emerald green and navy, matching its existing interface palette.

- `public/assets/brand/success-online-logo.png`: transparent full logo for the login page.
- `public/assets/brand/success-online-mark.png`: transparent compact emblem for desktop navigation, mobile navigation, checkout, and the browser icon.
- `public/brand.css`: responsive logo presentation.

Both PNGs were edited with the built-in imagegen tool from the user's supplied logo. The prompts were:

## Full logo prompt

Edit target: the attached Success Online Services logo. Use case: precise-object-edit / brand color adaptation. Preserve the recognizable interwoven sweeping S ribbon around a detailed globe, its overall silhouette, composition, and all exact brand text. Recolor the orange ribbon to rich emerald green (#078563, with subtle lighter #12a77e highlights) and the blue globe and lower ribbon to deep navy (#10243e) with restrained teal (#087d85) dimensional shading. Continents white. Replace the pale blue background with true transparency, including the open spaces inside the mark. Retain clean polished edges and legible bold lettering. Exact text underneath on two lines: 'SUCCESS ONLINE' and 'DIGITAL & ONLINE SERVICES'. SUCCESS emerald green, ONLINE navy, subtitle navy. No orange, no pale-blue background, no border, no new icons, no mockup, no extra wording. Keep the logo tightly composed with modest safe transparent padding. This is a real business website logo on white and pale mint interface backgrounds; maintain original brand identity with refined professional restrained dimensional styling. Deliver one transparent PNG.

## Compact emblem prompt

Precise edit of the supplied transparent emerald green and navy Success Online logo. Create the compact emblem asset for this same website: remove ONLY the two lines of text beneath the globe/S emblem. Preserve the emblem exactly: sweeping emerald S ribbon encircling the navy globe, white continents, all shape details and green/navy colors, no redesign. Reframe the existing emblem centered in a square canvas with approximately 6% transparent padding around its visible edges. True transparent background and preserve all transparent spaces inside the emblem. No text, no initials added, no new icons or border. This asset is for the website navigation and favicon and must be faithful to the original edited logo.
